Kare-Kare

Description

Kare-Kare is a rich and creamy Filipino peanut stew. Traditionally made with oxtail, it's thick, nutty, and best eaten with bagoong (shrimp paste) and lots of rice. It's a fiesta favorite.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg oxtail or pork hocks
  • 1 cup peanut butter, unsweetened
  • 1/4 cup ground rice or rice flour (for thickening)
  • 2 pcs eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bundle sitaw, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 bundle pechay
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups water
  • Bagoong for serving
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Boil meat: Boil oxtail in water for 1.5 to 2 hours until very tender. Reserve the broth. Set meat aside.
  2. Sauté: In a pot, sauté garlic and onion. Add the meat and cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Make sauce: Add 4 cups of the reserved broth. Stir in peanut butter until dissolved. Add ground rice to thicken. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Add vegetables: Add eggplant and sitaw. Cook for 5 minutes. Add pechay last and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Serve: Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve with bagoong and hot rice on the side.
