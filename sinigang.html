Pork Sinigang

Description

Pork Sinigang is the Philippines' most beloved sour soup. The tamarind-based broth is savory and sour, loaded with tender pork and fresh vegetables. It's the perfect comfort food on a rainy day.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or pork ribs, cut into pieces
  • 1 pack (40g) sinigang sa sampalok mix
  • 2 pcs tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 pcs labanos, sliced
  • 1 bundle kangkong
  • 5 pcs sitaw, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 2 pcs siling haba
  • 8 cups water
  • Fish sauce and salt to taste

Steps

  1. Boil pork: In a large pot, boil pork in 8 cups water. Remove scum that floats on top. Simmer for 45-60 minutes until tender.
  2. Add base: Add tomatoes and onion. Cook for 5 minutes until tomatoes soften.
  3. Sour it: Add sinigang mix. Stir and taste. Add fish sauce to adjust saltiness.
  4. Add vegetables: Add labanos and sitaw. Cook for 5 minutes. Add kangkong and siling haba last. Cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Serve: Serve hot with steamed rice and fish sauce with calamansi on the side.
