Pork Sisig

Description

Pork Sisig is a famous Filipino dish from Pampanga. It's made from crispy pork, onions, chili, and calamansi, served on a sizzling plate. It's sour, salty, spicy, and creamy all at once — perfect with rice.

This is the ultimate pulutan and ulam. Best served hot with a raw egg on top and extra calamansi on the side.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or pork maskara
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 pcs calamansi, juiced
  • 2 pcs siling labuyo, chopped
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 egg (optional, for topping)

Steps

  1. Boil the pork: In a pot, boil pork belly with salt, peppercorns, and bay leaves for 45 minutes or until tender. Drain and let it cool.
  2. Grill and chop: Grill or air-fry the pork until crispy on the outside. Chop into small bite-sized pieces.
  3. Sauté: In a pan, melt butter and sauté onion until soft. Add chopped pork and soy sauce. Cook for 3-5 minutes.
  4. Season: Add calamansi juice, chili, and mayonnaise. Mix well. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve: Transfer to a hot sizzling plate. Crack an egg on top if desired. Serve immediately with rice and extra calamansi.

Tips

For extra crispiness, let the pork cool completely before grilling. Don't overmix the mayonnaise — just enough to make it creamy.
